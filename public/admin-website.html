How this builder works. Your website is a page of blocks, top to bottom, plus a few pages of their own. The preview on the right redraws as you type - nothing is live until you click a card's Save.
  1. Your Website - the switch. Most facilities leave it on.
  2. Colors & Fonts - pick a preset or your own colors, and choose your fonts. It paints the whole site and the booking app; the preview shows every change at once.
  3. Hero - the first screen: headline, photos, the big button.
  4. Page Layout - every block in order. Tick Show to hide one without losing it, use the arrows to move it, and + Add a block for photo + text, FAQ, rates, testimonials, a video, a call-to-action, an inquiry form or a social row. Click a block to edit its words.
  5. Offer Slides, Steps, Staffed Days, Gallery, Events, Memberships, Promo, Contact - the words and photos of the built-in blocks. Where they sit and whether they show is set on Page Layout.
  6. Pages, Navigation, Search & Sharing, Site-wide - your own pages, the menu, what search engines and shared links show, analytics.
Photos are sized for phones automatically when you upload them. A gold badge means unsaved changes on that card.

Your Website

Most facilities build their website here. If yours has a website somewhere else, or does not want one, turn this off: visitors go straight to your booking page, the menu of marketing pages disappears, and everything below is kept for the day you switch it back on. Your booking page, memberships, gift cards and any pages you publish (terms, house rules) keep working either way.

Email Updates

Visitors leave an email and it lands HERE, on your device - never a mailing service. Switch it on and the signup shows on /updates (its own page, a hero button or menu item can point at it), on the holding page, and wherever you place the Email updates signup block on Page Layout. Each signup gets a confirmation through your email sender (Settings → Connections → Resend) with a one-click unsubscribe link; without a sender the list still fills. What's Public

Open your site one part at a time. Anything switched off is not yet public: its address shows your holding page instead (a custom page shows "not found"), and every menu item, hero button, footer link and section that points at it hides on its own. Nothing here needs a separate site or a re-install - flip the switch when you are ready. You and your staff keep full use of everything while signed in.
