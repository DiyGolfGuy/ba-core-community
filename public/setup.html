Step 1

What Kind of
Facility Is This?

Three answers shape everything after this — which questions you see, what your customers see, and how money and doors work. Answer for TODAY; every one of them can be changed later in Admin.

Who can book?
Members only — here is exactly how it works
• No member code, no booking. The server refuses any booking without a valid code — there is no way around it from the public page. You and your staff can always book anyone.
• Members pay two ways: their membership bills automatically through Stripe (monthly or yearly), and each booking charges their tier's per-30-minute rate — or nothing at all, if the tier includes free hours. You will set this on the Pricing step.
• New members join themselves. The booking page shows a Become a Member button that opens your online signup — perfect for linking straight from a main company website to this location.
• The club caps itself. Each tier has a Roster Cap you choose (75, 200, anything, 0 = unlimited); when it fills, online signup honestly says so.
• Everything else is identical — tee times, power automation, door codes, waivers all run exactly the same.
How do customers get in?
You picked Both — here is what that means
You will mark which days and hours are staffed on a calendar in Admin → Website → Staffed Days. Until a day is marked, the facility behaves as unmanned — your setup checklist will remind you. Two things follow automatically:

• Door codes always send, on staffed days too. Nobody is ever locked out because a member of staff stepped away.
• Deposits and settling at the door only happen during staffed hours. Money handled at the counter needs someone at the counter. Members still get their member price at any hour — outside staffed hours they simply pay all of it when they book, instead of some or all of it at the counter.
What does one booking reserve?
Entire facility — here is exactly how it works
• No bay choice on the booking page. Customers pick a date and time; the whole building is theirs for the session.
• One booking, all the automation. That single booking runs the full start sequence on EVERY bay — plugs, projectors, PCs — and the full end sequence on every bay when it is over.
• You keep per-bay control. The admin Bay Operations console still turns each bay on, off, or restarts it independently, any time.
• Pricing is for the building. Your base price per 30 minutes is the whole-facility rate ($60/hour for the building = enter 30). Per-bay premium prices never apply. With Members Only, the member rate is the whole-facility rate.
• Still add EVERY bay on the Bays step — each one needs its own hardware configured so the automation can run it.

Legal note. This software records signatures with a full evidence trail (name, IP, timestamp, browser, and a fingerprint of the exact document signed) and keeps them for your records retention period: 10 years at the least, which is BA's ruling as the product's owner, not the law — you can set it longer later under Settings > Protection > Records. It is not legal advice: have a lawyer licensed in your state write or review your waiver document. Questions to ask them: Does my waiver cover anyone who did not sign it, such as a member's guests? Can a parent or guardian sign for a minor where I operate? Is there anything my waiver cannot cover, however it is worded?
